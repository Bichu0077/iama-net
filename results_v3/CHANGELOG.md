## C. Headline results that CHANGED SIGN under the matched protocol

Old (confounded): M4 was the WORST model in-domain — mAP@0.5 0.7333 vs baseline 0.7527
(−1.94 pp; paired p=0.072; mAP@0.5:0.95 −1.44 pp, p=0.028 significantly worse), with
per-class collapses (crazing −10.4 pp, patches −13.2 pp, rolled-in scale −8.9 pp vs old M1 column).

New (matched, 100 epochs, seed 0, COCO init, no freezing): M4 is the BEST model —
mAP@0.5 0.734471 → 0.754711 (**+2.0235 pp, p=0.038**),
mAP@0.5:0.95 0.421198 → 0.425837 (+0.4638 pp, p=0.408 — inconclusive).
Per-class: crazing 0.345903 → 0.450471 (+10.5 pp), patches 0.941625 → 0.937919, rolled-in scale 0.611799 → 0.636919.

Reason: the old ablation fine-tuned a converged baseline for 25–30 epochs (10 frozen
backbone layers for M3/M4) against M1's 100 epochs — it measured fine-tuning damage, not
the interventions. Proof from the old artifacts themselves: the identity-mapped ANCHOR
(≈ M1 functionally, zero training) scored 0.7607–0.7608, above every trained variant.
